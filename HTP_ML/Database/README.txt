Element1–4(5): Quaternary or quinary constituent elements (equiatomic composition: 25–25–25–25 for quaternary, 20–20–20–20–20 for quinary).

afm: Magnetic state indicator (0 = ferromagnetic, 1 = paramagnetic).

lat: Crystal structure indicator (0 = body-centered cubic (BCC), 1 = face-centered cubic (FCC)). (Only the lowest-energy magnetic state and crystal structure among the four considered states were retained.)

sws0: Wigner–Seitz radius (volume-related parameter), optimized from ab initio calculations; unit: Bohr.

sws_init: Wigner–Seitz radius estimated from an empirical formula (used as the initial structure for calculations); unit: Bohr.

mag: Total magnetization; unit: μB per unit cell.

TC: Curie temperature; unit: K.

B0: Bulk modulus; unit: GPa.
